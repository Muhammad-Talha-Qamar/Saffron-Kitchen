Appitizers

1. Classic & Easy

  • Pigs in a Blanket: Wrap cocktail sausages in crescent roll dough and bake at 375*F for 12–15 minutes until golden.
  • Caprese Skewers: Thread cherry tomatoes, fresh mozzarella balls (bocconcini), and fresh basil leaves onto small skewers; drizzle with balsamic glaze
  • Stuffed Mushrooms: Remove mushroom stems and fill caps with a mixture of cream cheese, garlic, breadcrumbs, and parmesan; bake until tender
  • Bruschetta: Toast baguette slices, rub with garlic, and top with diced tomatoes, olive oil, basil, and balsamic vinegar
