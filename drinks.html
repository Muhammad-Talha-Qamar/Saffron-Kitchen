Drinks

Classic cocktails with clean ratios — shake, stir, or top with soda and serve.

Filter
Shaken Tequila

Margarita

Bright lime, triple sec, and a salted rim.

Shake 2 oz tequila, 1 oz lime juice, and 1 oz triple sec with ice. Strain into a glass with a salted rim.
Stirred Whiskey

Old Fashioned

Sugar, bitters, bourbon, and an orange peel.

Muddle a sugar cube with 3 dashes of Angostura bitters and a dash of water. Add 2 oz bourbon, ice, and stir. Garnish with orange peel.
Built Rum

Mojito

Mint, lime, white rum, and a splash of soda.

Muddle fresh mint leaves, 1 oz simple syrup, and 1 oz lime juice in a glass. Add 2 oz white rum, crushed ice, and top with soda water.
Stirred Gin

Negroni

Equal parts gin, sweet vermouth, and Campari.

Stir 1 oz gin, 1 oz sweet vermouth, and 1 oz Campari with ice. Strain into a glass over a large ice cube.
Stirred Gin / Vodka

Martini (Gin or Vodka)

Crisp, cold, and finished with olives or a twist.

Stir 2.5 oz gin or vodka and 0.5 oz dry vermouth with ice. Strain into a chilled martini glass and garnish with olives or a lemon twist.
Shaken Whiskey

Whiskey Sour

Bourbon, lemon, and simple syrup — bright and balanced.

Shake 2 oz bourbon, 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice, and 0.75 oz simple syrup with ice. Strain into a glass.
Built Vodka

Moscow Mule

Vodka, lime, and spicy ginger beer in a cold mug.

Combine 2 oz vodka, 0.5 oz lime juice, and 4–6 oz ginger beer in a copper mug or highball glass with ice.
Shaken Vodka

Cosmopolitan

Citrus, cranberry, and a clean martini-glass finish.

Shake 1.5 oz vodka, 1 oz orange liqueur, 0.5 oz lime juice, and 0.5 oz cranberry juice with ice. Strain into a martini glass.
Shaken Gin

Gimlet

Gin, lime, and simple syrup — sharp and refreshing.

Shake 2 oz gin, 0.75 oz lime juice, and 0.5 oz simple syrup with ice. Strain into a chilled glass.
