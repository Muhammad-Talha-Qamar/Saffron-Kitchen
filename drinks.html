Classic Cocktails

  • Margarita: Shake 2 oz tequila, 1 oz lime juice, and 1 oz triple sec with ice. Strain into a glass with a salted rim
  • Old Fashioned: Muddle a sugar cube with 3 dashes of angostura bitters and a dash of water. Add 2 oz bourbon, ice, and stir. Garnish with orange peel.
  • Mojito: Muddle fresh mint leaves, 1 oz simple syrup, and 1 oz lime juice in a glass. Add 2 oz white rum, crushed ice, and top with soda water.
  • Negroni: Stir 1 oz gin, 1 oz sweet vermouth, and 1 oz Campari with ice. Strain into a glass over a large ice cube
  • Martini (Gin or Vodka): Stir 2.5 oz gin or vodka and 0.5 oz dry vermouth with ice. Strain into a chilled martini glass and garnish with olives or a lemon twist
  • Whiskey Sour: Shake 2 oz bourbon, 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice, and 0.75 oz simple syrup with ice. Strain into a glass.
  • Moscow Mule: Combine 2 oz vodka, 0.5 oz lime juice, and 4-6 oz ginger beer in a copper mug or highball glass with ice.
  • Cosmopolitan: Shake 1.5 oz vodka, 1 oz orange liqueur, 0.5 oz lime juice, and 0.5 oz cranberry juice with ice. Strain into a martini glass.
  • Gimlet: Shake 2 oz gin and 0.75 oz lime juice with 0.5 oz simple syrup and ice. Strain into a chilled glass
