Main Courses

  • Air Fryer Bang Bang Salmon Mix mayonnaise , sweet chili sauce, and Sriracha for the sauce. Spread on salmon fillets and air-fry at 400°F (200°C) for ~10 minutes until caramelized. Serve with microwaveable steamed green beans and rice.

  • Crispy Cheesy Chicken Patties (approx. 30 min): Mix diced chicken breast with eggs, grated cheese, onion, ranch seasoning, mayo, and flour. Shape into patties, pan-fry until golden, and serve with a salad.

  • Creamy Tuscan Chicken (approx. 25 min): Sauté chicken, remove, then simmer garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, and heavy cream. Add pasta or simmer with orzo for a one-pan meal

  • Pakistani Aloo Gobi (approx. 30 min): Sauté onions and ginger-garlic paste, add spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander), potatoes, and cauliflower. Cook covered on low heat until tender, garnished with coriander

  • One-Pot Mexican Chicken and Rice (approx. 30 min): Brown chicken, then add rice, salsa, chicken broth, black beans, and taco seasoning. Simmer until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.

  • Easy Creamy Sausage Pasta (approx. 25 min): Brown Italian sausage, mix in garlic, marinara sauce, and cream. Stir in cooked pasta and top with cheese.
